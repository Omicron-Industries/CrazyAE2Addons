# Insane AE2 Addons

Insane AE2 Addons is the endgame half of Crazy AE2 Addons.
It adds mob storage, programmable builders, NBT-aware buses, and multiblocks that go from a spawner
in a box up to a contained black hole.

Everything here needs an existing ME network, and most of it needs the research system first.

## Feature Index

---

### Research and Progression

The gate in front of the advanced recipes, and the tool that makes big multiblocks buildable.

- [Research System](insaneguide/research.md)  
  Research Stations, Pedestals, and Research Units that turn computation and coolant into
  unlock keys on a Data Drive.

- [Multiblock Builder](insaneguide/multiblock_builder.md)  
  A tool that builds the structure around any multiblock controller, taking materials from your
  inventory or a linked ME network.

---

### Mob Storage

Mobs as a resource type: captured, stored in cells, released, or processed into drops.

- [Mob Storage](insaneguide/mob_storage_cells.md)  
  Mob Storage Cells, the Mob Cell Housing, and the Mob Key Selector used to fill mob config slots.

- [Mob Annihilation Plane](insaneguide/mob_annihilation_plane.md)  
  A part that captures living mobs in front of it into the network.

- [Spawner Extractor](insaneguide/spawner_extractor.md)  
  A multiblock built around a vanilla spawner that suppresses it and inserts its mob into the
  network instead.

- [Mob Farm](insaneguide/mob_farm.md)  
  A multiblock that simulates killing stored mobs and returns their drops and XP Shards, with
  looting and experience upgrades.

- [Mob Export Bus](insaneguide/mob_export_bus.md)  
  An export bus that spawns stored mobs back into the world.

- [Mob Formation Plane](insaneguide/mob_formation_plane.md)  
  A formation plane that spawns mobs the moment they are inserted into the network.

---

### NBT Filtering

Tools that filter items by their NBT data instead of by exact stack.

- [NBT Matcher](insaneguide/nbt_matcher.md)  
  The expression syntax shared by all three NBT tools.

- [NBT View Cell](insaneguide/nbt_view_cell.md)  
  A View Cell that filters the ME Terminal by an NBT expression.

- [NBT Storage Bus](insaneguide/nbt_storage_bus.md)  
  A Storage Bus whose partition is an NBT expression.

- [NBT Export Bus](insaneguide/nbt_export_bus.md)  
  An export bus that exports everything matching an NBT expression.

---

### Building and Automation

- [Auto Builder](insaneguide/auto_builder.md)  
  A programmable builder block that places and breaks blocks from a copied or hand-written pattern,
  pulling materials from the network.

- [Auto Builder Language](insaneguide/auto_builder_language.md)  
  The instruction set used by Builder Patterns.

- [Entity Ticker](insaneguide/entity_ticker.md)  
  A cable part that force-ticks the machine in front of it, up to 512 times per tick.

- [Ampere Meter](insaneguide/ampere_meter.md)  
  An inline block that measures FE or EU throughput and emits an analog comparator signal.

---

### Crafting and Patterns

- [Auto Enchanter](insaneguide/auto_enchanter.md)  
  An enchanting machine that spends XP Shards and XP fluids from the network.

- [Player and Automation Cards](insaneguide/provider_cards.md)  
  Upgrade cards that restrict a Crazy Pattern Provider to player-started or machine-started
  crafting jobs.

- [Entropy Cradle](insaneguide/entropy_cradle.md)  
  A multiblock accumulator that transmutes a block structure into a single powerful block when it
  discharges.

---

### Endgame Power

- [Energy Storage](insaneguide/energy_storage.md)  
  Ten tiers of AE energy cell blocks, from 8 MAE up to 2 TAE.

- [Reinforced Matter Condenser](insaneguide/reinforced_matter_condenser.md)  
  Compresses AE2 Singularities into Super Singularities.

- [Portable Penrose Sphere](insaneguide/penrose_sphere.md)  
  A contained black hole that converts Singularities into power, balanced between injection,
  heat, and mass, with a meltdown if you get it wrong.
