### Ever wished you could store your dog inside your AE2 system? Now you can! Maybe wanted to make a black hole in Minecraft and use it to harvest unimaginable amounts of power? Maybe you are just want to filter things in your system based on their NBT data? Or maybe you just wanted to have 2,2T AE stored in one block? Insane AE2 Addons brings you features no one ever imagined.

### Highlights:

*   Massive multiblock structures capable of generating insane amounts of power, farming mobs, and much more.
*   Entity Ticker - speed up processing like never before (comes with a great electricity bill).
*   Mob Storage Cells - store thousands of living entities in your ME Network. Pair them with digital mob farm multiblocks for maximum chaos. They also work with pattern providers now!
*   Automatic Enchanting - turn all that extra liquid XP into guaranteed enchantments. Full automation supported!

### Prefer the technical side of things?

All features are simple to use, but if you want, you can go deep. Crazy AE2 Addons includes:

*   An automatic builder - write your own scripts, or copy entire structures.
*   NBT view cells, NBT Storage Bus with advanced matching logic.

### Everything is documented in-game

No wikis required - the **AE2 Guide** has it all. Recipes are fully integrated into JEI/EMI with detailed previews. Multiblock controllers even include a preview for easy building, and there is also multiblock builder item to make things even easier. And if you ever need more help, just join my [Discord](https://discord.gg/mWy8AVRtwz).

### Compatibility

Insane AE2 Addons plays nicely with many AE2 expansions and popular mods:

*   Advanced AE
*   Extended AE
*   Expanded AE
*   Mega Cells
*   Modern AE2 Additions
*   Apotheosis (Auto Enchanter supports its enchantments)
